# AQDesk — User Manual

For everyone who uses AQDesk: visitors, members, experts and
administrators. Part B is the guide for **form creators and respondents**.

Every page works with a keyboard, a screen reader and with JavaScript
switched off. Menu names below are as they appear in the left-hand menu
(on a phone, open it with the ☰ button at the top).

**Demo accounts** (password `password`): `admin@aqdesk.test` (administrator),
`expert@aqdesk.test` (expert), `member@aqdesk.test` and `sara@aqdesk.test`
(members).

## Part A — Using AQDesk

### 1. Getting started

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Create an account | **Create account** (bottom of the menu) → name, email, password → accept the terms → confirm the link sent to your email. Until you confirm, you can browse but not post. |
| Sign in with Google or GitHub | **Sign in** → *Continue with Google / GitHub* (shown when the site has it set up). |
| Forgot your password | **Sign in** → *Forgot password?* → **Email me a reset link**. The link works once, for 60 minutes. |
| Sign out | The ⏻ button beside your name at the bottom of the menu. |

Five wrong passwords lock sign-in for one minute.

### 2. Accessibility options

Open **Display options** (the toolbar button at the top of every page):

- **Text size** (Default, Large, Larger), **High contrast**, **Readable
  font** (helps with dyslexia and low vision), **More line spacing** and
  **Reduce motion**. Choose and press **Apply**; **Reset** goes
  back to the defaults. Signed in, your choice is saved to your account;
  otherwise to this browser.
- The sun/moon button switches between light and dark themes.
- **Skip to content** is the first thing the Tab key reaches.
- The **Accessibility statement** (`/accessibility`) explains what is
  supported and how to report a barrier.

### 3. Your dashboard, profile and settings

- **Dashboard** — your numbers, a **To do** list (e.g. "accept an answer on
  your question") and recent activity.
- **My profile** — Overview, Questions, Answers and Activity tabs, your
  reputation, badges and privileges. Edit your about text and expertise there.
- **Account settings** (`/settings`, from your account menu) — which
  notifications also come by email, who can see your profile (everyone or
  signed-in members), and where you are signed in (**sign out everywhere
  else** needs your password).
- **Notifications** — the bell at the top shows new ones and updates itself.

### 4. Community questions and answers

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Ask | **Questions** → **Ask a question** → title, topic, details (Markdown toolbar; *Preview* tab), tags → **Post question**. |
| Answer | Open a question → write below → **Post your answer**. |
| Vote | ▲ / ▼ beside a post. Downvoting needs 15 reputation. |
| Accept | On your own question, **Accept** the answer that solved it. |
| Comment | *Add a comment* under a post (needs 5 reputation on other people's posts). |
| Find | Tabs **Newest · Trending · Most votes · Unanswered**, search, and **Tags** to follow topics. |
| Save | The bookmark button; find it later under **Saved**. |
| Report | **Report** on a post that breaks the Community guidelines (`/guidelines`). |

Edits keep earlier versions: "view N earlier versions" shows what changed.

### 5. Knowledge Hub and Learning Hub

- **Knowledge Hub** — articles and step-by-step guides by verified experts.
- **Learning Hub** — learning paths (ordered steps) and a resource library.
  Open a path and tick **Done** on each step; your progress bar fills up.
- **Become a verified expert** — apply at `/expert-verification` with your
  field, credentials and evidence. An administrator reviews it. Once
  approved you get the *Verified expert* badge and can write articles and
  curate paths.

### 6. Opportunities and organizations

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Browse | **Opportunities** — tabs for jobs, internships, scholarships, fellowships, grants, hackathons and calls for papers. |
| Apply | Open one → **Apply** → cover letter and CV (PDF/DOC, kept private). Follow it under **My applications**: Pending → Reviewed → Shortlisted → Accepted, with any message from the reviewer. |
| Job alerts | On the board, search then **Create alert**; you are told when a new match is published. |
| Publish (experts) | **My postings** → new opportunity → **Publish opportunity**. Optionally under your organization (**Organizations** → **Create profile**). |
| Review applicants (experts) | **My postings** → applicants → open one → **Save status** with an optional message; private notes are only seen by reviewers. |

Opportunities close by themselves when the deadline passes.

### 7. Projects

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Start | **Projects** → **New project** → **Create project**. You are the owner. |
| Invite | In the project, **Invite someone** → email and role: *collaborator* (can add files, tasks, feedback) or *viewer* (can only look). They accept with **Accept and join**. |
| Files | **Upload a file**; a newer copy goes in with **Upload version**, and every version stays downloadable. Files are private to the project. |
| Feedback | **Feedback** tab → start a thread → **Post feedback**; others **Post reply**; the starter marks it resolved. |
| Tasks | **Tasks** tab → **Add to To do**, assign someone, set priority and due date, and move it To do → In progress → Done. |

### 8. Events

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Find | **Events** — Upcoming / Past, in person / online / hybrid. |
| Register | Open an event → **Register** (answer the organizer's questions if there are any). When it is full, **Join the waitlist**; you are told if a seat frees up. |
| Join an online event | The **Join now** button appears on the event page 15 minutes before the start (seat holders only). |
| Calendar | **Add to calendar** downloads an `.ics` file, or use the Google Calendar link. |
| Cancel | **Cancel my registration** (or **Leave the waitlist**) so the seat goes to the next person. |
| Organize (experts) | **Events** → **New event** → **Save draft** → **Publish**. Add a joining link or let AQDesk create a free Jitsi room. See **Attendees** (and download them as CSV). Reminders go out the day before and an hour before. |

### 9. Podcasts

Listen on **Podcasts**: every episode has a player, show notes and a full
**transcript**. Podcast apps can subscribe with the series' RSS feed.
Experts create a series (**New series**), then **New episode** with audio
(upload or link) and a transcript; an episode cannot be published without
a transcript.

### 10. Consulting

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Find a consultant | **Consultants** — search by name or topic; each card shows the next free time. |
| Book | Open a profile → pick a free time → **Book this time** → topic and message → confirm. Booked times disappear for others, and you cannot double-book yourself. |
| Your sessions | **My sessions** — *I booked* / *Booked with me*. **Join now** opens the private video room 10 minutes before. **Cancel session** with a reason before it starts. |
| Offer sessions (experts) | **Consultants** → set up your profile (`/consulting/profile`), then **My availability**: weekly hours and days off. |
| Clients (consultants) | **My clients** — people who book appear automatically; **Add a client** for others. Notes on a client are private to you. |
| Proposals (consultants) | On a client's page, **New proposal** → title, what you will do, price, valid until → **Save draft** → check it → **Send to …**. A client on AQDesk answers it themselves; for anyone else, record their answer. When the work is done, **Mark the work as completed**. |
| Proposals (clients) | **Proposals** in the menu, or the notification → read it → optional note → **Accept** or **Decline** before its "valid until" day. |

### 11. Newsletter and updates

**Newsletter** in the menu (`/newsletter`): enter your email, tick the
agreement and press **Subscribe**. Visitors get an email with a link to
confirm (it works for 7 days); nothing is sent before that. Signed in, your
own account email is subscribed at once. Every newsletter ends with
**Unsubscribe with one click**; mail apps that show their own "Unsubscribe"
button use the same link.

Announcements from the AQDesk team appear as a banner at the top of every
page (News, Good news or Important). Close one with its **×** button; it stays
closed. **Updates** in the menu lists the current and earlier announcements.

### 12. Privacy and your data

**Privacy & my data** (`/privacy/my-data`): see your consents, **Download
my data** (everything AQDesk holds about you, as a file), or **Delete my
account** (needs your password; your posts stay as "Deleted user", and
upcoming bookings are cancelled with notice to the other side).

### 13. Administration (administrators)

| Menu | Use |
|---|---|
| **Users** | Find people, change their role, deactivate or reactivate accounts. |
| **Roles & permissions** | Tick which role may do what (e.g. *Organize events*). |
| **Reports** | The moderation queue: open a report, decide (dismiss, or remove the content with a reason); every decision is in the moderation log. |
| **Moderate questions / opportunities** | Close, reopen or delete any post. |
| **Expert verification** | Approve or reject requests (a rejection needs a reason). |
| **Activity log** | Who did what, when (kept for a limited time). |
| **Newsletter** | Subscriber numbers and campaigns. **New campaign** → subject and message (Markdown) → **Save draft** → send yourself a test → **Schedule** (date, time and time zone) or **Send now**. Emails go out from the queue in batches; the campaign page shows how many were sent. A scheduled campaign can go back to draft with **Cancel the schedule**. **Delivery report**: sent, failed (with the reason), skipped and opened; **Retry failed** sends only to the addresses that failed. |
| **Announcements** | **New announcement** → title, message, optional link, kind, who sees it (everyone, members or visitors), whether it can be closed, and when it shows (from / until, in a time zone) → **Post announcement**. The list shows which are live, upcoming or ended and how many members closed each. |

## Part B — Forms: guide for creators and respondents

AQDesk's Form Builder makes surveys, sign-up sheets, feedback forms and the
registration questions for events.

### B1. For form creators

You need the *Build forms* permission (experts and administrators).

1. **Create the form.** **My forms** → **New form** → title and description
   → **Create and add questions**. The form starts as a **Draft**; *only you
   can see it until you open it*.
2. **Add questions.** Under *Add a question* choose a **Type**, write the
   **Question**, an optional **Hint** (read out to screen-reader users with
   the field), tick **Required** if needed, and for choice types list the
   **Choices** one per line → **Save question**.

   | Type | Respondent sees |
   |---|---|
   | Short answer | One-line text box |
   | Paragraph | Multi-line text box |
   | Email address | Email box (checked for a valid address) |
   | Number | Number box |
   | Date | Date picker |
   | One choice (radio buttons) | Pick one |
   | Several choices (checkboxes) | Pick any |
   | Dropdown | Pick one from a list |
   | Rating 1–5 | Five labelled options |
   | Consent / agreement | One checkbox they must tick |

   Use **Edit** to change a question, the move up / move down buttons to
   reorder, and delete to remove it.
3. **Settings.** *Who can respond* (anyone with the link, or **only
   signed-in members**), **one response per member**, a **thank-you
   message**, and **closes automatically** at a date and time →
   **Save settings**.
4. **Check it.** **Preview** shows the form exactly as respondents will see it.
5. **Open it.** **Open for responses**, then share the link (`/f/…`; the
   address cannot be guessed). **Close** stops new responses; **Back to
   draft** hides it again for changes.
6. **Read the answers.** **Responses** shows a **Summary** per question
   (counts for choices, average for ratings) and **Individual responses**,
   newest first. **Download CSV** gives a spreadsheet (one column per
   question).
7. **Use it for an event.** When editing an event, pick the form as its
   *registration questions*; everyone who registers answers it, and the
   answers appear on the attendee list and its CSV.

**Delete form** removes it with all responses (you are asked to confirm).

### B2. For respondents

1. Open the link you were given (or **Register** on an event that has questions).
2. Fill in the fields. Required ones are marked; hints sit under the
   question. Everything can be done with the keyboard: Tab to move, Space to
   tick, arrow keys within a group of choices.
3. Press **Submit**. If something is missing or wrong, the page lists the
   problems at the top and beside each field, and keeps what you typed.
4. You see the creator's thank-you message. If the form allows one response
   per member, opening it again tells you that you have already answered.

A form that is closed or past its closing time says so instead of showing
the questions.

## Getting help

- Something does not work or a page is hard to use: see *Tell us about a
  problem* on the **Accessibility statement** page — use the **Report** link,
  or ask in **Questions** under the tag `accessibility`.
- Content that breaks the rules: **Report** on the content.
- Setting AQDesk up: `docs/SETUP-GUIDE.md` (local) and `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`
  (server).
